# Shield Media

A Plex and live TV app for the NVIDIA Shield and other Android TV boxes.

- **Plex**: plays your movies and shows at full original quality (4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, lossless audio) with no transcoding.
- **Live TV**: add your own IPTV provider (Xtream Codes login or an M3U link) for channels, a TV guide, movies and series.
- **Blu-Ray room**: a cinema-style browsing mode for Plex libraries with "Blu-Ray", "4K", "UHD" or "Remux" in their name.
- **Commercial cover (beta)**: during live TV ad breaks, it shows a "short commercial break" screen with a small live preview. You can turn this off in Settings.

The app doesn't include any content or IPTV service. You sign in with your own Plex account and add your own provider.

## Install on a Shield

1. On the Shield, open the **Google Play Store** and install **Downloader** (by AFTVnews).
2. Allow Downloader to install apps: **Settings → Device Preferences → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader**.
3. Open Downloader and type this address into the URL box:

   ```
   https://github.com/penwal/shield-media/releases/latest/download/ShieldMedia.apk
   ```

4. Press **Go**. When it finishes downloading, choose **Install**.
5. Open **Shield Media** from your apps row. It shows a code; go to **plex.tv/link** on your phone and enter it.
6. To add live TV, open **Live TV**, choose **Add a source** and enter your provider's details.

## Updating

Enter the same Downloader address again and install. That link always gets the newest version, and your sign-ins and settings are kept.
